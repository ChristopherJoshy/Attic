Our Story

Built from the Ground Up

What started as an empty patch of land near the Vattolikadavu bridge became a shared dream. Through sleepless nights, setbacks, and relentless passion, PLANEAT rose — a restocafe built from containers, fueled by fire, and made for people who love real food.

Every dish carries the warmth of charcoal, the richness of slow-cooked spices, and the pride of a team that refused to give up.
